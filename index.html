SmokeReset Privacy Policy

By Pranav Dev. Effective 13 October 2026.

SmokeReset is a self-help companion for adults aged 18 and over quitting cigarettes or vaping. This policy describes the Android app, package com.pranavdevapps.smokereset.

Records stored on your device

The app stores your journey type and quit date, optional usage and spending details, chosen reasons and custom reason, streak history, lapses and close calls with optional triggers, craving exercise times and ratings, chosen actions and outcomes, and reminder preferences. Records are kept in local SQLite storage and used to show progress, estimates, history and your selected reminders.

No automatic uploads

SmokeReset has no user account, server, advertising, analytics or tracking SDK. It does not automatically upload journey records, device identifiers or diagnostic reports to Pranav Dev. Production error logging is disabled. Device-generated record identifiers are used locally. Reminders are scheduled by your device; the app does not register for remote push notifications.

Reminders and permissions

Notification permission is requested only when you choose to enable reminders. You may disable them in Settings or Android settings. Quiet hours and discreet wording are available. Android may delay reminders. Notification contents may be visible on your lock screen depending on your device settings.

Retention, backup and security

Records remain on your device until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Android cloud backup is disabled. The app does not separately encrypt its SQLite database; your device's access controls and security protections apply. Protect your device and any exported copies. Changing phones does not automatically transfer your journey.

Export and deletion

Settings → Privacy, export and delete lets you create a readable JSON copy and open your device's share sheet. You choose the destination. The file may contain sensitive behavior information; the recipient or service you choose handles it under its own privacy rules. Export copies can remain in app cache until cleared by the app or operating system.

Delete all data removes the app's journey records and settings, cancels scheduled reminders and attempts to remove export copies in its cache. It returns the app to onboarding. This cannot delete files already shared or saved outside the app; remove those separately. No developer-held journey database exists to delete.

External support resources

Opening a support link loads the named provider's website in a browser. The provider may receive ordinary web request information, such as your IP address, and use its own cookies and privacy policy. SmokeReset does not attach your journey records to support links. Those providers are independent and their inclusion does not imply endorsement.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include through our email provider. We use it to respond and retain correspondence only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Please avoid sending health records. Contact us to request deletion of correspondence when it is no longer needed; we cannot delete messages retained in your own mailbox.

Adults and health information

SmokeReset is intended for adults aged 18 and over, not children. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. It does not replace a qualified health professional.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its effective date if app behavior changes. For privacy or support questions, contact Pranav Dev at pranav.labs.dev@gmail.com.
